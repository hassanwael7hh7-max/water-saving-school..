The challenge

Where does the water go?

Water is wasted when taps are left running, leaks go unnoticed and rainwater drains away unused. We can design and manage our school building to prevent waste and reuse water safely.

Smarter buildings

3 ways to save water

🚰

1. Install smart taps

Sensor or push taps stop automatically. Low-flow aerators reduce the amount of water used while keeping handwashing practical.

🔧

2. Detect leaks quickly

Inspect taps, toilets and pipes regularly. Leak sensors and a simple reporting system help caretakers fix problems before lots of water is lost.

🌧️

3. Harvest rainwater

Collect suitable roof rainwater in covered tanks and use it for gardens where local rules allow. Keep tanks maintained and water separate from drinking supplies.

Extra building idea: Add water meters to different areas. If water use suddenly rises, staff can investigate and find hidden leaks sooner.

Our goal
